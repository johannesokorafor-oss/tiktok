How publishing works

The final MP4 is uploaded through the official TikTok Content Posting API into your inbox as a draft. You then open the TikTok notification, review and edit the post and press Post yourself — this app never publishes for you. The generated cover is embedded into the beginning of the video so it can be selected as the TikTok cover during that editing flow; the Upload/Draft endpoint does not accept a separate cover image or a caption, so paste the caption below.
