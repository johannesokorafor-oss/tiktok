How publishing works

The final MP4 is uploaded through the official TikTok Content Posting API into your inbox as a draft. You then open the TikTok notification, choose your own cover, review the caption and press Post yourself — this app never publishes for you. The Upload/Draft endpoint accepts neither a cover image nor a caption, so the caption is prepared here for you to paste.
